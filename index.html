Chocolate Avocado Mousse

A creamy, decadent vegan dessert that's healthy and easy to make. Serves 4.

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe avocados, pitted and peeled
  • 1/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/4 cup maple syrup (or agave)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt
  • Optional: Fresh berries for topping

Instructions

  1. Add all ingredients to a blender or food processor.
  2. Blend on high until smooth and creamy, about 1-2 minutes. Scrape down sides as needed.
  3. Taste and adjust sweetness if desired.
  4. Transfer to serving bowls and chill in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes.
  5. Serve topped with fresh berries or nuts.

Prep Time: 10 minutes | Chill Time: 30 minutes | Total Time: 40 minutes
